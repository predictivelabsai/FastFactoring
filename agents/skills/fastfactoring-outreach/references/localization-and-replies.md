# Localization and reply handling

## Enabled launch locales

English/UK, Estonian, German, French, Swedish, Latvian, Norwegian, Danish, Polish, Dutch, Finnish, and Lithuanian. Russian and Uzbek may remain in source code but are disabled and must not receive campaigns.

## Localization packet

For each locale produce:

1. subject and body in the target language;
2. literal English back-translation;
3. terminology choices for invoice, debtor, receivable, factoring, payer, and supplier;
4. formal/informal register decision;
5. localized opt-out sentence and recognized opt-out phrases;
6. native reviewer identity/date;
7. approved claims and forbidden claims.

Do not translate company names, personal names, citations, or product names. Keep **FastFactoring** for the open-source project and **Factorio** for the reference demo.

## Five-touch pattern

- Day 0: one observed business hook and one relevance hypothesis; ask for a brief conversation.
- Day 3: add vertical-specific evidence or a workflow question.
- Day 10: offer a concise open-source/reference-demo insight; no pressure.
- Day 17: share a distinct implementation or payer/supplier angle; at most one CTA.
- Day 28: close the loop politely; no CTA and no guilt language.

## Reply classes

Recognize localized variants of positive interest, question, referral, timing/later, not relevant, opt-out, out-of-office, and bounce. Opt-out, complaint, bounce, and do-not-contact are terminal. Out-of-office pauses until the stated return date. All substantive replies are drafted for human approval; never negotiate financial terms automatically.
